FAQs

Questions about aerial roof measurements?

Some quick answers to help you understand the service.

What does RoofArts measure?

RoofArts is positioned to provide roof, wall, siding, gutter and broader exterior measurement reports, along with takeoffs from architectural plans depending on the service selected.

Who can use aerial roof measurement reports?

Roofing contractors, siding and gutter installers, insurance professionals, restoration teams, builders, property managers and homeowners may find measurement reports useful.

What information can a roof report include?

A roof report may include roof area, squares, facet breakdowns, pitch information and edge lengths such as ridges, hips, valleys, eaves and rakes.

Can measurements be prepared from blueprints?

Blueprint-based takeoffs can be used to organize roof and exterior dimensions from architectural plans. Availability depends on the project information supplied.

Do aerial measurements replace an on-site inspection?

No. Aerial measurements are a planning and estimating aid. Image quality, obstructions and property conditions can affect what is visible; verify critical dimensions on site where needed.
